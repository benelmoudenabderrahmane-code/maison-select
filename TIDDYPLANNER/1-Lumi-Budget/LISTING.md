## FAQ (Etsy "Frequently asked questions" section)

**Do I need to pay monthly?** No. Pay once, use it as long as you like.

**Does it work on my phone?** Yes — open your private link and choose "Add to Home Screen". It works on iPhone and Android.

**Can I use it on my phone and computer at the same time?** Yes, but data doesn't sync automatically. Use Settings → Back up on one device and Restore backup on the other.

**Is my data safe?** Your numbers never leave your device. There's no account and no server.

**Can I change the currency?** Yes, 24 currencies are built in.

**I'm new to budgeting. Is it too complicated?** It's made for beginners: a 3-minute setup, a checklist, and sample data you can explore first.
