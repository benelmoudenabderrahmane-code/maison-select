Good to know

🔒 Your data is private

Everything is saved inside your own browser on your own device. There's no account and nothing is sent to a server.

🗂️ Back it up

Settings → Back up saves a small file. Keep it in your cloud drive or email it to yourself. Use Restore backup to bring it back.

📱 Switch devices

Make a backup on device A, then open your link on device B and choose Restore backup. Data doesn't sync automatically.

⚠️ Don't lose your data

Clearing browsing data or using private/incognito mode can erase saved data. On iPhone, Safari can also clear a website's data after 7 days without a visit, so add the planner to your Home Screen and back up once in a while.
